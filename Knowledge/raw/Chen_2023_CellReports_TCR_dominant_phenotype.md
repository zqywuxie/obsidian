# TCR sequences are the dominant factor contributing to CD8+ T cell phenotype

> Chen DG, Xie J, Su Y, Heath JR. *Cell Rep.* 2023;42(11):113279.
> DOI: [10.1016/j.celrep.2023.113279](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2023.113279)
> PMID: [37883974](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37883974/) | PMCID: [PMC10729740](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10729740/)

---

## Abstract

Antigen-specific CD8+ T cells mediate pathogen clearance. T cell phenotype is influenced by T cell receptor (TCR) sequences and environmental signals. Quantitative comparisons of these factors in human disease, while challenging to obtain, can provide foundational insights into basic T cell biology. Here, we investigate the phenotype kinetics of 679 CD8+ T cell clonotypes, each with specificity against one of three immunogenic viral antigens. Data were collected from a longitudinal study of 68 COVID-19 patients with antigens from severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2), cytomegalovirus (CMV), and influenza. Each antigen is associated with a different type of immune activation during COVID-19. We find TCR sequence to be by far the most important factor in shaping T cell phenotype and persistence for populations specific to any of these antigens. Our work demonstrates the important relationship between TCR sequence and T cell phenotype and persistence and helps explain why T cell phenotype often appears to be determined early in an infection.

## Key Findings

### 1. Study Design
- **68 COVID-19 patients** — longitudinal samples
- **679 CD8+ T cell clonotypes** with known antigen specificity
- **3 viral antigens**: SARS-CoV-2, CMV, Influenza — each associated with different immune activation patterns during COVID-19
- Measured **phenotype kinetics** over disease course

### 2. Core Discovery
| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| TCR sequence is the **dominant factor** shaping CD8+ T cell phenotype | TCR > environment for determining T cell fate |
| Effect consistent across all 3 antigens | Generalizable, not antigen-specific |
| Even **bystander T cell phenotype** is dominated by TCR sequence | Not just antigen-experienced cells |
| Phenotype largely determined **early in infection** | TCR-intrinsic programming |

### 3. Quantitative Comparison
- Systematically compared the relative contributions of:
  - TCR sequence
  - Environmental signals (cytokines, inflammation, tissue)
- TCR sequence **overwhelmingly dominated** over environment for:
  - Phenotype (effector, memory, exhaustion markers)
  - Persistence (long-term clonotype maintenance)
  - Functional responses

### 4. Clinical Relevance
- Helps explain why T cell differentiation trajectories are established early
- Suggests TCR-based predictors of T cell behavior may be feasible
- Provides foundation for TCR-sequence-based therapeutic targeting

## Methods Highlights

- **scRNA-seq + TCR sequencing** from longitudinal COVID-19 patient cohort
- Known antigen specificity via tetramer/dextramer sorting for 3 viral epitopes
- Phenotype kinetics tracked across disease course
- Computational modeling to disentangle TCR vs. environmental contributions
